# Ferret

## The problem
You use your credit cards every day, but you have no clear idea where the money goes. Typing every purchase into a spreadsheet or app is a chore, so it never happens. Your statements already list every transaction, but they come as locked PDFs or buried in emails, and they don't add up to a picture of your spending.

## How it helps
- Drop in a statement PDF or connect Gmail once, and Ferret reads your statements for you. No typing.
- Enter a statement's password once; after that, Ferret opens that card's statements on its own.
- See what you spent this month, on each card, and at which merchants, with every transaction listed.
- Every statement is checked so the totals add up before anything is saved, and the file is deleted as soon as it has been read.

## Who it's for
- People with one or more credit cards who want to know what they spend without doing the bookkeeping.
- Anyone whose statements arrive as password-protected PDFs or by email.
- People who like asking an AI chatbot questions: it can look up your spending in Ferret, read-only.
