# Ferret: guide

## Getting started
1. Open the Ferret website in your browser. There is nothing to install, and it's free.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**, or type your email and choose **Email me a sign-in link**, then open the link from your inbox within 15 minutes. If you're new, this creates your account; there is no Ferret password to remember. (S01)
3. You land on an empty Spending page that says "Let's find your spending". Choose **Connect Gmail** (recommended) and allow Ferret to read your mail. It only looks for statement emails from supported banks, and it can't change or send anything. (S02)
4. Ferret takes you to Statements and starts looking for your statements. If you'd rather not connect Gmail, choose **Choose a PDF** instead and pick a statement your bank sent you. (S03)
5. If a statement is locked, Ferret asks for its password once: type the password your bank set for statement PDFs (not your online banking password) and choose **Open and save**. From then on, that card's statements open by themselves. (S04)
6. Watch the statement move from "Reading…" to "Reconciled". Your spending now shows on the Spending page. (S03, S02)

## How to use it

**See what you spent**
1. Choose **Spending** in the sidebar. You see this month's total, how it compares with last month, and how much went on each card. (S02)
2. Use the month picker and the card filter at the top to look at another month or just one card. (S02)
3. Look at **Top merchants** to see where most of the money went, and scroll to **Transactions** for every purchase, newest first. Type a merchant name or an amount in the search box to find one. Refunds are marked "Refund" and taken off the total. (S02)

**Bring in a new statement**
1. With Gmail connected, there's nothing to do: new statements come in each month on their own. To look right away, go to **Statements** and choose **Check now**. (S03)
2. To add a statement by hand, drop the PDF onto the Statements page or choose **Choose PDF**. Ferret reads the file, checks the totals, then deletes it. (S03)
3. If a row says "Needs password", choose **Enter password** and type it. The statement waits for up to 7 days; the row shows the date. If you don't want it, choose **Skip this statement** and the file is deleted. (S03, S04)
4. If a row says "Didn't add up", nothing from that statement was saved and the file is gone. Choose **Report problem** to flag it (only the bank, month and totals are sent), and **Upload again** later. (S03)

**Ask an AI chatbot about your spending**
1. Go to **Settings** and find **AI chatbots**. (S05)
2. Choose **Copy** next to the server address, then **Create token** (or **New token**). Copy the access token shown and choose **I've saved it**; it is shown only once. (S05)
3. Add Ferret to your chatbot's connections using that address and token, then ask it things like "How much did I spend at Carrefour in October?". It can read your transactions and totals, and can't change anything. To stop it, choose **Revoke**. (S05)

You can also replace or remove saved statement passwords, disconnect Gmail or delete your account in **Settings**. (S05)
